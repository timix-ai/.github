**Timix.AI** helps IT services, law firms, consultancies, agencies, and accounting
firms turn the hours their teams work into accurate bills and real profit:

- **Hierarchical time tracking** — Customer → Project → Task → SubTask, with every hour logged at the SubTask level.
- **Real profitability** — dual cost and bill rates that roll up to per-client margin.
- **Budgets that stop overruns** — soft and hard limits with live threshold alerts.
- **Billing you can trust** — Time & Materials, Fixed Price, and Overhead; approvals; and client invoicing.
- **Bring your own AI** — a built-in AI assistant and an official **MCP server**, so you can run Timix.AI from Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor.
- **Bilingual** — English and Hebrew (right-to-left).

### Open-source integrations

- **`timix-mcp`** — connect Timix.AI to any MCP client (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor): connection details, tool catalog, and the official registry manifest.
- **`timix-zapier`** — the official Zapier integration, wiring Timix.AI into thousands of apps.
